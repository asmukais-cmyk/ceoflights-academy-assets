Benefit Reference — What Each One Actually Covers
Ticket Protection Plus (TPP) — 8% of ticket, min $59.95 /person
✈️
Connections
What it covers: If the customer misses a connection due to a delay or weather, we rebook them at no charge — no airline fees, no agency fees. Subject to seat availability in the same booking class.
⏱️
Delays & Cancellations
What it covers: If the flight is cancelled or delayed 3+ hours, we handle the rebooking — our agency fees are waived, but fare differences may apply depending on seat availability in the same booking class. And if they arrive 3+ hours late because of the airline, we file a compensation claim on their behalf.
💰
Price Drop Protection
What it covers: We monitor the fare for 24 hours after booking. If it drops, the customer gets a voucher for the difference — valid for a full year.
⭐
Priority Support
What it covers: 24/7 priority access to our support desk — seat assignments, special meals, any changes — they go straight to the front of the line.
🏥
Illness*
What it covers: If they get sick and can't travel, we go directly to our airline contacts and advocate on their behalf for a refund or credit. They need a doctor's note. Important: The refund is not guaranteed — the airline reviews each case and makes the final decision based on their own policy. Our job is to fight for the best outcome, but we cannot promise a specific result. How to sell it: "We go to bat for you with the airline" — sell on our advocacy, not on a guaranteed refund.
Flexible Ticket (FT) — 6% of ticket, min $149.95 /person
📅
Date Change
What it covers: We waive our agency fee and the airline change fee is also waived — but the fare difference still applies if the new fare is higher. It depends on seat availability at the time of the change. Key conditions: First change only. Must be requested at least 72 hours before departure. Same airline and route only. Changes must be made through us — not directly with the airline. Important: Do NOT promise the change is always free. If seats are only available in a higher fare class, the customer pays the difference.
* Illness disclaimer: When a customer can't travel due to illness, we contact the airline directly with a doctor's note and advocate for a full refund or credit. However, the final decision always rests with the airline based on their policy. Some airlines grant full refunds, others offer credits or exchanges only. Sell on our advocacy, not on a guaranteed outcome. Say: "We go directly to the airline and fight for the best result" — never promise a specific refund.
